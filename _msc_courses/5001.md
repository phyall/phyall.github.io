Syllabus
------


**Module 1:** Mechanics of a system of particles in vector form. Conservation of linear momentum, energy and angular momentum. Lagrangian formulation and the action
principle: Configuration space, generalised coordinates. Constrained systems, motion in a central field, including
Kepler problem, Rutherford scattering, Small oscillations, normal modes. (12L+3T)

**Module 2:** Rigid body kinematics and dynamics:Kinematics, rotational kinetic energy,moments of inertia, inertia tensor, Euler angles, angularmomentum, Free rigid body motion. (8L+2T)

**Module 3:** Hamiltonian formulation:The Legendre transformation, canonical momentum Poisson brackets, Canonical transformations. (9L+3T)

**Module 4:** Dynamical Systems – First order systems , Second order systems, Linear Stability analysis, Classification of Fixed points, Newtonian systems -Phase space reduction, cyclic coordinates, Phase space description and evolution, periodically driven systems (9L+3T)

**Module 5:** Relativistic mechanics- Space-time, 4-vectors, Lorentz transformations, basic relativistic kinematics and dynamics (5L+2T)
