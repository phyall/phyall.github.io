Syllabus
------


**Module 1:** Hydrogen atom and its eigenvalues. Spherical Harmonics. Fine and Hyperfine structure of H atom. Atomic structure of two electron system : He. Many electron system : Hartree-Fock method, L-S coupling, JJ coupling. Interaction of electromagnetic field with atoms: Zeeman, Stark and Paschen-Back effects (17L)

**Module 2:** Electronic structure of H2 molecule: LCAO, LCMO method. Molecular spectroscopy (electronic, rotational, vibrational): Microwave spectroscopy, Infra-red spectroscopy, Raman spectroscopy. Resonance spectroscopy: Nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR), Electron Spin Resonance (ESR). X-ray spectroscopy (15L)

**Module 3:** Lasers: Principles of laser action in atoms and molecules. Einstein coefficients, population inversion, two and three level systems. He-Ne laser and other inert gas laser, atomic halogen lasers (10L)
