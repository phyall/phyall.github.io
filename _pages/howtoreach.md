IIT Palakkad currently operates from two campuses -- Nila Campus, and Sahyadri Campus. The two campuses are side-by-side, 
and the offices of the members of the Department as well as the facilities are distributed over both campuses. Nila and 
Sahyadri campuses are connected to the Coimbatore International Airport through the NH544. Nearest railway stations to 
IIT Palakkad are the Palakkad Junction Railway Station, known as the Olavakkode Junction, and the Palakkad Town Railway Station.
