IIT Palakkad currently operates from two side-by-side campuses -- Nila Campus, and Sahyadri Campus. The offices of the members of the Department are located in Sahyadri, while the facilities are distributed over both campuses. The address to the Department is as follows.

- Palakkad Town Railway Station

**Closest Airport:** Coimbatore International Airport (CJB) -- 1.5 Hrs along NH544. 

**Alternative Airport:** Kochi International Airport (COK) -- 2.5 Hrs along NH544.
