Faculty in the Department of Physics perform state-of-the-art research in diverse directions. The set of broad research directions existing in the Department are as follows.  

- Astrophysics (Theory)
- Condensed Matter Physics (Experiment)
- Condensed Matter Physics (Theory)
- High Energy Physics (Experiment)
- High Energy Physics (Theory)
- Quantum Information Theory
- Soft Matter and Statistical Physics (Experiment)
- Soft Matter and Statistical Physics (Theory)
- String Theory

See [here](topic_wise.md) for a research area-wise grouping of faculty.
