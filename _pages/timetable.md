**Important notes:** 

- Numbers in slot names refer to the day of the week i.e., 1=Monday, 2=Tuesday, etc. Example: in slot PA5, 5 stands for Friday. Similarly, in M3, 3 stands for Wednesday.
- A, B, C, D and H are 3 credit slots (3X50 mins each)
- F, G, I, J, K, and L are 3 credit slots (2X75 mins each)
- E and M are a 2 credit slot (2X50 mins)
- Q and R1 - R5 are 1 credit slots (50 mins each)
- PM1 - PM5 are morning lab slots of 2hrs 45mins (9:00 hrs to 11:45 hrs)
- PA1, PA2, PA4, PA5 are afternoon lab slots of 2hrs 45mins (14:00 hrs to 16:45 hrs)
- PA3 is an afternoon lab slot of 1hr 50 mins (14:00 hrs to 15:50 hrs)
