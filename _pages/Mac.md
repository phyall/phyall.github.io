The M.Sc. curriculum provides students with a strong foundation in modern physics while exposing them to cutting-edge topics 
through carefully designed courses. It also fosters a research-oriented mindset through project-based learning. The curriculum 
is reviewed and updated regularly to keep pace with emerging developments in physics and the evolving needs of the academic and 
scientific community.
