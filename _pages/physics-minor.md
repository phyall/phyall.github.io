The Minor in Physics consists of a basket of courses designed by the Department of Physics. A student pursuing BTech in IIT Palakkad is awarded a
Minor in Physics if the student earns a minimum of 12 credits (outside the PME and PM Core) by taking courses from this basket. For details, check the
[Regulations for UG Program](https://iitpkd.ac.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/2b5d5c85-1eef-4125-bf0f-693361c74aef.pdf).
