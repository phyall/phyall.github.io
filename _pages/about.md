The Department of Physics at IIT Palakkad was established in August 2015 and is actively engaged in teaching and research at the forefront of both experimental and theoretical physics. We invite you to explore our website to learn more about the Department's activities, academic programs, research areas, faculty members, and ongoing research projects.

{% include slideshow.html %}


The Department currently offers a postgraduate program (M.Sc. in Physics) and two research programs (M.S. by Research and Ph.D.). The M.Sc. curriculum provides students with a strong foundation in modern physics while exposing them to cutting-edge topics through carefully designed courses. It also fosters a research-oriented mindset through project-based learning. The curriculum is reviewed and updated regularly to keep pace with emerging developments in physics and the evolving needs of the academic and scientific community.
