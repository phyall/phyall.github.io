The Department of Physics at IIT Palakkad was established in August 2015 and is actively engaged in teaching and research at the forefront of both experimental and theoretical physics. We invite you to explore our website to learn more about the Department's activities, academic programs, research areas, faculty members, and ongoing research projects.

The Department of Physics at IIT Palakkad, active since August 2015 in both experimental and theoretical research, is currently engaged in teaching and research at the forefront of experimental and theoretical physics, reflecting the institute's stated purpose to create knowledge, communicate knowledge, and apply knowledge for the benefit of society. Its faculty pursue work spanning Quantum Physics, Theoretical Astrophysics, String Theory, Soft Matter Physics, Experimental Biophysics, and Condensed Matter Physics — research that aligns with the institute's ambition to be a globally recognized institution at the forefront of education, research, and technology development, with strong ties to industries and other academic institutions. Through its two-year Master's programme, which offers advanced theoretical and experimental training aligned with current global research trends and gives students hands-on exposure to cutting-edge research through short-term and extended projects, the department also embodies the institute's broader commitment to nurturing lifelong learners with a rigorous foundation, a passion for innovation, strong ethical values, and a recognition of their social responsibility. In this way, the Physics Department's own trajectory — building research strength while training the next generation of scientists — mirrors IIT Palakkad's institute-wide vision and its motto, "Nurturing minds for a better world."

The Department currently offers a postgraduate program (M.Sc. in Physics) and two research programs (M.S. by Research and Ph.D.). The M.Sc. curriculum provides students with a strong foundation in modern physics while exposing them to cutting-edge topics through carefully designed courses. It also fosters a research-oriented mindset through project-based learning. The curriculum is reviewed and updated regularly to keep pace with emerging developments in physics and the evolving needs of the academic and scientific community.
